## Requested amendment

Authorize at most **72 additional short synthetic user prompts total** across
HOTR-12-LAMPREY and HOTR-12A through HOTR-12K, including failures and retries.
This is a shared cap, not 72 per application. At most twelve may be spent on
Lamprey and at most eight on any other individual application. Preflight must
pass before each application's first model prompt. Stop an integration after
two consecutive model failures, preserve evidence and repair the cause before
spending more prompts. Record the chosen model/provider before the request;
no automatic provider substitution or new paid accounts, purchases or models.

Each user instruction is synthetic and at most 8 KiB. A turn has a 180-second
deadline and at most eight tool calls. Use provider output limits where the
actual application exposes them; do not claim a dollar ceiling that its
subscription/API billing cannot enforce. Model inference may incur the owner's
existing provider charges. Log reported model usage without credentials.
Fuzz, load, crash and soak campaigns remain local and spend no cloud prompts.

The original twelve prompts per later milestone and all resource, preservation,
personal-file selection and deployment gates remain in force. No real context
may be sent to a cloud provider through this amendment.

Approval must be recorded from the user's actual reply before exceeding the
original twelve-prompt ceiling. This proposal does not approve itself.
